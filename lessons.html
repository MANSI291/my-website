Cyber Ethics & Safety Lessons

Follow these important principles to become a responsible digital citizen.

🔑

Strong Passwords

Use unique passwords containing a combination of uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers and symbols.

🔒

Protect Privacy

Do not share sensitive personal information such as passwords, addresses or financial information with unknown people.

🎣

Avoid Phishing

Be careful with suspicious emails, messages and websites asking for personal information.

🤝

Respect Others

Do not cyberbully, harass or share another person's private information without permission.

📰

Verify Information

Check reliable sources before believing or sharing information on the internet.

💻

Use Technology Responsibly

Use digital technology for positive purposes and respect laws, rules and other users.
